## What expiry day changes
- **Theta is brutal.** Premium bleeds all day; a correct direction held too long still loses. Monday's staircase — holding for hours as price grinds up — is the pattern least likely to pay today.
- **Pinning is real.** Price often gravitates to the strike with the most open interest as the afternoon wears on. With 23,400 and 23,500 both heavy, chop between them is the base case.
- **Trade earlier, not later.** The last hour on expiry is where premium collapse does the most damage to buyers.
- Consider **ITM strikes** if you do trade: less theta per point, as the lesson on premium selection covers.
