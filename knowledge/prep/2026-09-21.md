## Reward to risk, from these levels
The box is about 100 points tall. Entering near an edge with a stop 15–20 points beyond it gives the other edge at roughly **5R**. The same trade taken from the middle of the box offers about 2R at best, and needs a stop that's easy to hit. This is the whole reason to wait for the edges. The scanner's first five sessions point the same way: most of the profit came from letting trades run to about 3R, and stops placed where the idea is wrong did better than tight ones.
