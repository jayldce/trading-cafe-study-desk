## What changed in the playbook
- **B got its two biggest Sensex wins (+98.08, +150.64).** Both were pullbacks he chose over the breakout candle: "I'd rather have a breakout followed by a good pullback". [▶ 10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLu3xXwwUCQ&t=4920s)
- **New disqualifier for pullbacks:** skip a breakout out of a stuck 3–4-candle cluster when the breakout or follow-up candle needs a 50–67-point stop. [▶ 10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLu3xXwwUCQ&t=4740s)
- **New disqualifier for sweeps:** a tiny stop at the day low is not enough. He wants strength at the turn, or "it will keep hitting again and again". [▶ 11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLu3xXwwUCQ&t=9960s)
- **A number on the stop cap:** a ₹300 option should carry at most a 25–35-point stop, and a stop above ~10% of the premium "makes no sense". His own sheet doesn't keep to the 10%: three of his five directional wins had stops of 12–18% of entry. [▶ 09:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mLu3xXwwUCQ&t=3240s)
- **D again showed the confirmation-vs-aggressive split on one zone:** confirmation won +56.25 and +94.43, and the pin-bar-high attempt lost −23.90.
- **Contradiction:** 26-Oct's "one stop per zone" against today's same-zone re-entry, which paid +94.43 eight minutes after a stop.
- **New concepts:** *Double bottom: wait for the second leg's panic*. The 74,000 call writers were added to *Trapped writers*, and there is a far-limit-order broker-glitch test.
