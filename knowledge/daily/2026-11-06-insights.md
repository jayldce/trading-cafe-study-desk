## What changed in the playbook
- **New don't: no counter-trend puts on a trend day.** "Put is not part of my setup right now." He called the 24550 PE's slide from ~400 to ~90 a falling knife, and the one put he took lost.
- **New risk rule: decide the trade count first and split the risk equally.** Three trades, the same rupee risk on each, and no extra lots after a win.
- **"A consolidation break is not the pullback"** is now a disqualifier on the higher-high concept. He refused four continuation breaks (10:07, 13:58, 14:07, 14:27) because their stops were 15–18 points against 8–11 for his pullbacks.
- **New concepts:** *shake-out before the breakout* (a strong candle under the base takes the late buyers' stops, and the entry comes on the reversal back above it), *who is trapped: sellers inside a red-volume up candle* (300 sell against 180 buy and the candle still closed up; he uses it as confirmation only), and Trade Circuit's *a global sell-off is not a short signal*.
- **Setup A gained a loss:** a follow-up put in the midday lull, the window he calls his weakest.
