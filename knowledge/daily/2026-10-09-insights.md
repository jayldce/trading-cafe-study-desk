## What changed in the playbook
- **New concept: enter above the higher high to shrink the stop.** He waits for price to clear the rejection candle's high, so the stop sits under the last small candle instead of under the whole structure.
- **New risk rule: no re-entry on a failed breakout's pullback** unless the candle was very strong. Wait for a fresh higher high. [▶ 10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9kn5B0LK1YM&t=3780s)
- **New disqualifiers:** for a pullback, "no green candle at the zone, no entry". For a reversal, "one candle is not a reversal": it needs a clear break or a completed pattern.
- **More evidence:** A (follow-up/hard trigger) got +20.17. D (confirmation reversal) got +13.81. A− took two more losses, ~10 points each.
- **Trade Circuit (guest section):** with-trend calls paid and both counter-trend puts lost. New guest concepts cover counter-trend trades kept tiny, the FVG limit re-entry and the Friday weekly-candle fight.
- **Contradiction logged:** his pre-open "shorts are trapped" read was right about direction, but he bought two puts against it (1W / 1L). The entry form decided the result, not the read.
