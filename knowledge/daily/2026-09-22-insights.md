## 5. What changed in the playbook
- **Setup A (consolidation breakout with follow-up), reinforced twice in one morning on the same level:** a raw poke above 158 trapped and stopped at 144; the follow-up entry above 159 minutes later cleared target 1. The playbook's core finding (follow-up candle vs raw poke) keeps holding.
- **Setup D (base reversal), new sub-pattern named:** he introduced "first bounce" — the very first return to a level right after it breaks — as a small-risk reversal zone. It lost today (~−6), so it's logged as a variant that still needs the usual confirmation, not a free pass.
- **Setup E (jodi), best construction yet + a new caution:** the 66-combined jodi (+18, ~27% RoI) is now the cleanest documented example. A *second* same-day jodi built 30 minutes later into already-elevated IV (86 combined) sat in a paper loss before an even bigger whipsaw hit — new nuance: a jodi built after IV has already jumped costs more and loses the "cheap straddle" edge.
- **Setup G (Telegram calls), rule held under pressure:** a quantified spoken claim ("200 points in 10 minutes") still isn't enough without a frame of the channel's own outcome. Logged unclear, same as always.
- **Verification catch:** the sub-agent's first draft logged a win on a 23400 PE breakout plan (58 trigger) that the transcript shows never actually triggered — he says outright at 14:48 it "didn't run" above 58. Removed from the trade log entirely; kept in the playbook as a reminder of exactly the mistake the project's verification step exists to catch.
- **Correction (23-Sep):** an earlier version of this note called today a Wednesday that broke the Wed/Thu-Sensex pattern. 22 Sep was a Tuesday, so Nifty was the expected index.
