## What changed in the playbook
- **Disqualifier added to base reversals (D).** The confirmation form failed when the "base" was a shallow W inside a put still falling with the index's climb (−29 in two minutes). Confirmation does not turn a pause into a bottom. [▶ 10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=5820s)
- **Third visit to a zone, said outright.** Pullbacks one and two are entries; on a third return "the market does not want to go up". He also said: "don't enter on a breakout retest". [▶ 11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=7920s)
- **Cluster rule (barcode concept).** Don't trade inside 3–4 candles hugging a level; wait for the break. Sensex needs a 70–80-point move to pay a normal stop, so 15–20-point scalps inside a range are not worth it. [▶ 11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=7080s)
- **Theta as a time stop.** A pullback that sat 11 minutes without a second candle was cut near cost on expiry eve. [▶ 14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=19140s)
- **New concept:** *Who is trapped: the first breakout of a sideways open.*
- **Contradiction:** the third A− win in 21. It came in the same breath as "we wanted small candles first", and again on a one-way move.
