## What changed in the playbook
- **New risk rule (§3): an expiry-day risk gate.** When stops come out at 50–90 for 70–100 of reward, skip the trade. Take entry, stop and target from the **option's** own prices, never from spot. At 14:47 spot candles turned green while the option did not move. Both trades he took that day with stops of about 55 points or more lost.
- **Jodi (E):** his first jodi stop "in 2–3 months" (−100). New disqualifier: a squeeze is necessary but not sufficient. On an eroding expiry, with a zone that keeps rejecting price, the squeeze can last until the close.
- **New concept:** *Who is trapped: put writers under an absolute zone on expiry* (82,000). It explains why premiums stayed dead until that zone broke. It describes the mechanism; it does not forecast the break.
- **Evidence added:** A +96 (hard trigger), B −55.51 (a stop twice his usual Sensex ceiling), H −29 (a rolled strike that bought a higher high 3 minutes before the day's high), D one unclear.
