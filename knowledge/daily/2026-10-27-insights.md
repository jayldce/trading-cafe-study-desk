## What changed in the playbook
- **The risk gate, in numbers.** He wants 8–10 points of risk for 15–20 of reward. A stop of 17–24 points for 19–30 of reward is "not even 1:2", and he skips it. [▶ 10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az8Ezg6JWks&t=4380s)
- **New pullback disqualifier.** If the pullback never comes, don't take the higher high, because the stop is now too far for the setup to be valid. [▶ 10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az8Ezg6JWks&t=4800s)
- **The W trigger, named.** A higher second bottom, then the neckline break, then a small candle that holds above it. Not the neckline-break candle itself if that candle is huge. [▶ 09:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az8Ezg6JWks&t=1920s)
- **New concepts:** *Dead-cat bounce, then continuation* and *Pick the leg with the better reward-to-risk*.
- **Cheap jodis only.** He waited from 10:35 (legs at 45–47) and 12:45 (30–35) and built jodis only at 68 and 41 combined.
- **Contradiction recorded:** his "OI is manipulated" sits against the trapped-writers reading in the concepts. Both agree on one point: read OI after a move, never before it.
