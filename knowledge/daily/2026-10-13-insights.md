## What changed in the playbook
- **New risk rule: size the stop before the entry.** If the structure needs 25–28 points, wait for a pause that gives 10–15. He also passed on 1:1.7 setups ("not even 1:2"). This was folded into the concept *Stop size comes from structure*, now written out with qualifiers and disqualifiers.
- **New risk rule: no averaging on expiry day.** Out-of-the-money options decay too fast, so take the stop and wait for a fresh setup.
- **New concepts:** *Day-high break after a squeeze* and *No averaging losses on expiry day*. Also added: a 5-minute-analysis / 1-minute-execution note under *Scalper entry vs traditional entry*.
- **New disqualifiers:** a zone that has already given its bounce gets no re-entry (*Bounce strength*, 10:50 and 11:18). Don't reach for a pin bar while price is still running into the zone. Midday breakouts need a shakeout first.
- **More evidence:** H +108.5 (two wins, both after the stop shrank), D +34.6, B +30.28, E +34 across two cheap jodis (both booked short of target, as usual).
