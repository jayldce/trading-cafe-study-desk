## 0. Daily routine
1. **Global cues before the open:** US close, Asia (Nikkei/China), WTI crude key zone, bond yields, gold/BTC. They set expectations for volatility, not direction. Rising crude is read as bearish for India. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=180s)
2. **Higher-timeframe Nifty zones:** mark tested daily zones and the next support/resistance to estimate how many points are "on the table". [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=480s)
3. **Pick the index, then the strikes:** focus on whichever index (Nifty or Sensex) has its weekly expiry next [▶ 22-Jul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCedaeF9RWg&t=3840s). After the open, pick one CE and one PE, each around **₹150 premium** (near-ATM/ITM), charted side by side with spot. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=780s)
4. **Read IV level and skew:** for Nifty, 12–13 is normal, 15–16 is costly, 27+ is abnormal. **For Sensex, 18–20 is normal** [▶ 3-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=14040s). Also compare call-side vs put-side IV; the richer side is where sellers expect movement. [▶ 11-Sep level](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=1560s) · [▶ 7-Sep skew](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=12120s) · [▶ 8-Sep skew](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=13620s)
5. **Option chain as context, not trigger:** PCR, max pain, and OI build-up or unwinding at round strikes. Read OI as the **net change over the last ~30 min**, not a snapshot; intraday OI isn't a leading indicator. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=13500s) · [▶ 4-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=6780s)
5b. **No price-based indicators.** RSI, moving averages and stochastics are noise to him, and RSI divergence is among the lowest-probability trades. If he uses anything it's volume-based (VWAP, volume, ADX). On a 1-min chart the **option leads and spot lags**, so execute off the option chart. [▶ 3-Sep indicators](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=6960s) · [▶ 3-Sep option leads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=4260s)
6. **Stay flexible:** trade whichever side confirms, whatever your bias. *Evidence: on 11-Sep his bias was bearish but the best trade was a CE; on 8-Sep the confirmed put side paid while forced calls chopped; on 15-Sep, a gap-up day, he still armed both a CE and a PE from the open, and five separate CE strike rolls (23400→23350→23250→23200→23350) never filled while both PE trades ran 80–150+ points — his own EOD line: "ओनली पुट चल रहा है" (only PUT worked).* [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=5940s) · [▶ 15-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=18960s)

## 1. Mark levels on the option chart itself
- **Base zone** (where it launched from), **day high/low**, and **barriers** with repeated rejections, which make a **make-or-break** zone. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=1260s)
- Map key **spot** levels (round numbers like 23,300 or 23,800, prior S/R) onto the option chart for targets. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=4800s)
- Zones where lower lows form become **supply**; the option's previous launch zone is **demand**. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=4260s)

## 2. Setups (Chinmay)

### A. Consolidation breakout with follow-up entry ⭐ core setup: 20W / 6L / 3 unclear, Nifty +330 · Sensex +482, 28/29 verified
- **Context:** price keeps attacking the same barrier, or sits in a tight "barcode" of small, identical candles (§4). The third attempt is often the one that works.
- **Avoid:** the *first* breakout candle, because breakout candles often trap. *Evidence: entering directly on it went 0W / 10L (see A−).*
- **Entry:** directly above the **follow-up candle** after the breakout candle. Alternative: a **hard trigger price** just above the hurdle, entered only when it actually trades there ("trap the last sellers"). [▶ 7-Sep trigger](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=16560s)
- **SL:** below the base or consolidation low; the follow-up entry is what makes the SL small. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=3660s)
- **Targets:** the next option-chart zone that corresponds to a spot S/R level, then the next level.
- **The cleanest proof (6-Aug, from his own trade journal):** on the *same* level he first bought the raw breakout (306.4 → stopped, **−8.4**), then re-entered on the follow-up seven minutes later (310.8 → 368, **+57.2**). Same idea, same level, one candle of patience apart. [▶ 6-Aug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRl6SfYXXiQ&t=1680s) · frame `data/frames/VRl6SfYXXiQ/030453.jpg`
- **It still fails sometimes (6 losses, 3 scratches):** when momentum doesn't extend after the break, it just stops out (21-Aug Sensex PE −21, 20-Aug CE −18). A 20/26 hit rate is an edge, not a certainty.
- **How strictly it's counted:** only entries where he explicitly waits for the follow-up candle, or names a hard trigger price above the hurdle, count here. Plain candle-close entries are tracked separately as setup H. He teaches the follow-up entry live on 29-Jul: the breakout candle couldn't hold and printed a pin bar, so only work above the attempt once the follow-up comes (+32, from his journal) [▶ 29-Jul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWD9ZCXlChk&t=2160s). 30-Jul's hard-trigger version: a squeeze with a trigger at 266, SL 259, skip it if price dips below 240 first (+57) [▶ 30-Jul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5uyR5XEbx4M&t=7380s).
- **Warning sign:** if spot keeps moving but the option **doesn't follow**, the trade is weak; tighten or exit. *The one loss (8-Sep 10:02) did exactly this.* [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=3960s)
- **Hard-trigger version, with a stepped trail (17-Sep):** on the 74100 CE he offered two entries — a low-risk one at the 350 base, or "above 376" — and drew the box at the trigger (375.5, SL 322.49). It peaked near 535; he raised the trail 404 → 420 → 449 → 482 and the 482 trail was hit: **+106.5** from the trigger. [▶ 17-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=njvG_Pr9CpM&t=2640s)
- **Hard-trigger version can fail in opening-range chop (18-Sep):** on the 23300 CE he named the trigger in advance ("above 124", SL 116, targets 136 / 156) after a few small candles under it, but the whole Nifty morning was a 30–40 pt range with the lowest delta he'd seen in a month. The CE rejected within two minutes and the 116.58 stop was hit: **−7.59**, the first loss for the hard-trigger version. He told viewers to keep a strict stop, not to re-enter, and to wait for a clear break of the range either way. [▶ 18-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=2100s)
- **Evidence:** 18-Sep 23300 CE trigger 124.17→SL ❌ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=2100s) · 11-Sep 182→210 ✅ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=3780s) · 7-Sep 136.9→~165 ✅ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=2640s) · 7-Sep 149→165 ✅ · 8-Sep 161→~172 ✅ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=1200s) · 8-Sep 182→~199 ✅ · 8-Sep 180→SL ❌ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=3840s) · 4-Sep 136→149 ✅ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=1860s) · 3-Sep (Sensex) 77200 PE ~377→~511, taught live as "the textbook one" ✅ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=3300s) · 3-Sep 76600 CE hard trigger 303→~336 ✅ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=1860s) · 3-Sep 76400 CE ~310→340 ✅ · 15-Sep 23650 PE, trigger 172 named in advance above a barrier rejected 2–3 times, trailed to 259 (+87, confirmed by frame) ✅ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=1140s) · 22-Sep 23300 CE, the same level twice in one morning — a raw poke above 158 trapped and stopped at 144, then the follow-up entry above 159 minutes later cleared T1 (173) with the 175.38 high confirmed on frame ✅/❌ [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dpx4i4TCkEc&t=1440s)

### B. Pullback to breakout zone (first green candle): 14W / 0L / 3 unclear, Nifty +139 · Sensex +462, 14/17 verified
*3-Sep: a single-candle entry at the 300–309 bounce zone on the 77000 PE went straight to target (389).* [▶ 3-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=11820s)
- **Context:** after a strong move breaks a zone, wait for price to return to that zone.
- **Entry:** the **first green candle that touches the zone**. Enter above its high, SL at its low; a small pin bar in the zone is ideal. Late-day versions used just 3–5 pt risk.
- **Rule:** only trust a pullback after an **aggressive prior move**. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=2280s)
- **18-Sep 23400 PE (+~12.5):** planned before it happened ("a pullback candle we planned; the pullback candle came"): after a ~30-pt run he refused to buy at 150 (reward:risk not favourable) and waited for the dip to ~145, entered on the first green candle with a ~4–5 pt risk, was +15 within three minutes and trailed 154 → 159. The 169 target never printed (high ~167.6), so the 157–159 trail exited it for a small win: the setup's risk was tiny but the reward was capped by a momentum-less day. [▶ 18-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=1080s) · frames `data/frames/EPh5qxWEUhU/002130.jpg`, `003100.jpg`
- **Nuance:** he says he *used to* buy the first green candle after every pullback, but once a range has broken and shows continuation he prefers waiting for the deeper pullback zone. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=3780s)

### C. Liquidity-sweep reversal ("bottom buying"): 3W / 2L / 4 unclear, Nifty +26 · Sensex +42
After price takes out the prior zone's low and a strong bullish close appears, enter on the first reversal sign with the SL at the day low. The same stop-hunt can happen **on the option premium itself** (a "shakeout" dip); wait it out before entering.
**When it fails:** if the "sweep" is really price continuing into a news event, it goes straight through the stop. That's how the one loss happened, on SBI results (7-Aug, −6) [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ayYEP_dKaLY&t=17700s). A sweep needs stops to be taken and then *rejected*, not a fresh reason to keep falling.
**18-Sep (unclear):** after the 23250 CE swept the day low he framed a bottom buy only because the risk (~6 pts) against the ~14-pt reward was about 1:2, and said he personally "didn't feel like" taking it; the CE ran to ~156 but it was offered to viewers with no fill, box or exit, so no points are logged [▶ 18-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=7260s). His filter for bottom buys is reward:risk, not just "liquidity was taken".
**Liquidity ≠ resistance:** he draws a line between a zone that merely holds and a zone where stop losses actually sit. Only the second is a sweep. [▶ 25-Aug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=12540s) [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=6240s) · [▶ 4-Sep win ~117→139](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=8940s) · [▶ 4-Sep shakeout](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=5220s)

- **21-Sep put (loss, ~−6, frame):** he waited for the day-low sweep and then took the 23500 PE from ~136 with the SL under 130; by 11:33 the PE was 127.4 and the stop level was gone, because Nifty was rising in a staircase that had begun off the 09:15 low. The sweep read was right about the *low* but the put was on the wrong side of the day's trend. [▶ 11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DrmIRaTPU-A&t=8220s)
- **Wait for the stops to actually be taken, not just for a fresh low** (reinforced 21-Sep). He refused to treat any bounce as tradeable until the day's low at 129 was swept, and only then looked for the reversal candle. [▶ 11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DrmIRaTPU-A&t=7800s)

### E. Jodi (long straddle): 8W / 2L / 3 unclear, Nifty +84 · Sensex +192, 10/13 verified
- Buy a near-ATM CE and PE together when a big move is expected but its direction isn't clear (expiry day, or a tight range about to resolve).
- Manage the **combined premium** as one position: combined SL (e.g. 52 → 27) and combined target (~85–100). Don't manage each leg separately.
- Expect a long, flat theta bleed before the payoff. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=19080s)
- **Construction (3-Sep, 25-Aug):** CE and PE around ₹70 each (~₹140 combined), or ~₹25 combined on expiry day. SL = half the combined premium, target ≈ double it. Book each leg on its own; don't trail a jodi. Only attempt it after ~1 PM, once premiums have cheapened. [▶ 3-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=16260s) · [▶ 25-Aug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=17940s)
- **When not to:** if IV is elevated, premiums stay too rich for a cheap straddle, so he skips it entirely (6-Aug) [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRl6SfYXXiQ&t=21960s). And it needs the range to actually break: on 18-Aug spot got pinned to 24,200 all afternoon and both legs bled (−10.5) [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=20220s). On 20-Aug he built the case all session and never pulled the trigger.
- **Where it has paid:** volatile expiry afternoons. 30-Jul ran two back-to-back jodis, each with its own strikes and levels (+30, then +45 on a 24-pt stop) [▶ 30-Jul](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5uyR5XEbx4M&t=19800s). 4-Aug is the most detailed live walkthrough: combined 45, SL 20, target 70–75, exited ~60 for about 33% on ₹3,000 [▶ 4-Aug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmnPW3HauhM&t=11340s). 15-Sep: 23250 PE + 23350 CE built at combined 47 once premiums had cheapened, confirmed touching the 70–75 target zone (+25) [▶ 15-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=15960s).
- **New timing nuance (15-Sep):** for a *second*, later jodi he specifically waited for the 3:15–3:30 PM closing-auction window, saying premiums stay flat/"stuck" until cash-session auction activity picks up, then move fast — a sharper version of "only attempt after ~1 PM" [▶ 15-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=21900s). That particular jodi's own outcome is logged `unclear`: he named a 75–85 combined zone as bookable and legs reached 63–64, but never stated an actual booked exit and explicitly deferred the "verified PnL" to later, so it isn't counted as a confirmed win.
- **…and it failed two days later (17-Sep, contradiction):** the same late "cash-session" idea on the 74300 PE + 74500 CE (combined 215, built at 15:14) went nowhere — spot didn't move, the premium stayed "dead", and he exited within minutes: "ये वाली फ्लॉप हो गई". The earlier jodi that day, built at 13:42 for 240 combined, made net 30. So the closing-window version is **1 unclear, 1 loss** so far — not yet a rule. [▶ the flop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=njvG_Pr9CpM&t=22860s) · [▶ his recap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=njvG_Pr9CpM&t=23160s)
- **Cleanest construction yet, on a news spike (22-Sep):** 23400 CE (34) + 23350 PE (32) = 66 combined, SL 35, target 65–80, all drawn on the chart in advance. An Iran/Russia war headline hit while he'd stepped away and the put leg exploded; the CE bled. Booked ~84 combined, **+18** (about 27% on deployed capital, frame-confirmed at 13:53). ### H. Breakout on a candle close, no follow-up wait: 10W / 4L / 4 unclear, Nifty +111 · Sensex +42
Entry as soon as a candle closes above the level, without waiting for the follow-up candle. It sits between A and A−: far better than poking in on the breakout candle itself (0W / 11L), but the sample is small and the wins are modest. ## 3. Risk and trade management
- **Risk first:** know your SL in points **and** in rupees per lot before entering. Minimum **1:2** risk-reward; comfortable risk is 4–10 pts. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=6360s) · [▶ 7-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=14820s) · 18-Sep: a reversal with a 13–14-pt stop needs a ≥30-pt rally to pay, so he waited for a tiny stop plus a trail; a bottom buy passed only at ~6 pts risk vs ~14 reward [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=5160s)
- **Trailing ladder:** once ~10 pts in favour, trail near entry. With 2+ lots, book ~1 lot at 1–1.5× risk, move the rest to breakeven, then trail under each fresh higher low. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=4200s) · [▶ 7-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=8340s) · 18-Sep: part-book near 160, stop to entry, then 154 → 159 (hit a few minutes after the 167 stall, small win kept) [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=1380s)
- **With one lot:** no part-booking possible. Take the full target or the full stop. *(Trade Circuit)* [▶ 10-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=19680s)
- **Accept the SL.** Cut failed breakouts fast and never widen the stop. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=4080s)
- **Roll strikes:** (a) after an SL, roll to a cheaper strike on the same side if the read still holds; the biggest win on 7-Sep came right after doing this [▶ 7-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=2340s). (b) When premium runs away or the option moves too sluggishly, roll to a nearer ~₹150 strike [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=4140s).
- **Cap the day at 2–3 trades.** After back-to-back stop losses, close the terminal instead of hunting a fourth trade. [▶ 25-Aug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=15887s) · 18-Sep: three trades, then he stood aside from ~11:00 to the 13:25 close ("if there's no strength on either side, don't force it") [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=15420s)
- **Hard SL ceiling on Sensex:** never more than 25–30 points (about ₹600–800 a lot). If the setup needs more room, skip it. [▶ 6-Aug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRl6SfYXXiQ&t=22080s)
- **A trade needs a placeable stop.** A good read with no sensible SL location isn't a trade. [▶ 11-Sep pt2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEYKvXFibAU&t=9900s)
- **He avoids Bank Nifty for scalping** (monthly expiry only, so intraday liquidity is thin). [▶ 6-Aug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRl6SfYXXiQ&t=720s)
  - **Contradicted 21-Sep:** he ran a full hard-trigger breakout on the BankNifty 56500 CE — trigger named hours ahead, entry above 470, SL 446, target 531 — and it was the day's cleanest trade (+61 pts). So the rule is "not usually", not "never": on a day when Nifty was dull he went where the movement was. [▶ 13:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DrmIRaTPU-A&t=15300s)
- **No pyramiding in scalps.** Adding quantity is only for swing positions. [▶ 7-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=10860s)
- **Big SL means small quantity.** [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=2040s)

## 4. Don'ts
- **Don't trade inside an absorption zone**, a range where price is soaking up supply or demand before its next leg. [▶ 4-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=7260s)
- **Don't trade on FOMO or anxiety.** His live reset: 10–12 slow breaths with eyes closed before looking at the chart again. [▶ 3-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=9960s)
- **Don't trade inside a "barcode"** (a run of tiny, identical candles at one level). It's a deadlock between buyers and sellers, so wait for it to break, then use setup A. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=3420s)
- **Don't buy a third test of a level** that has already bounced twice; it's weakening, so wait for the break. [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=6660s)
- **No bottom-fishing** inside a falling range; buy only at marked higher highs or confirmed reversals. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=4020s) · [▶ 8-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=4080s)
- **Don't chase or late re-enter** a zone that has already given its big move (7-Sep late re-entry: −3). After the 18-Sep 09:44 stop he told viewers "don't look for a re-entry here" and wait for the range to break clearly either way [▶ 18-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=2280s). [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=9960s) · [▶ 7-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=16620s)
- Don't trade the tight opening range after a huge gap; ignore the post-close cash candles. [▶ 11-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=1560s)
- **Don't fight glitches.** If the exchange or broker misbehaves (e.g. the 10-Sep BSE glitch), document it and move on; don't revenge-trade. [▶ 10-Sep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=19470s)

- **21-Sep, Bank Nifty:** the "avoids Bank Nifty" rule broke. See §3 — one clean hard-trigger breakout, +61 pts, on a dull Nifty day.

His end-of-day recap quoted "158 to 230" as one trade, which is the Telegram thread, not the charted entry. When a recap and a chart disagree, the chart wins.
- *"Small candles at a level → blast is coming"* (11-Sep) vs *"barcode = no-trade zone"* (8-Sep). These are consistent: the squeeze tells you a move is coming, but you trade the **break** (setup A), not the inside of the squeeze.
- *"Buy the first green candle at the pullback zone"* vs *"I used to do that, now I wait"* (8-Sep). The aggressive version seems reserved for zones after a strong move; the logged B trades were all small-risk and late in the day.
- *"Wait for the follow-up candle"* vs the 18-Aug **aggressive continuation entry** (F), which he took deliberately when no pullback came, and which worked. The distinction he draws: entering late into a *confirmed* trend is acceptable with a bigger stop; entering early on an *unconfirmed* break (A−) is not.
- *Liquidity sweeps are high-probability* vs the 7-Aug failure into SBI results. A sweep into news isn't a sweep.
- He **keeps a real trade journal** (Google Sheet "TRADE RECORDS", seen 6-Aug) with entry, SL, target, exit and net result per trade, including losses. Where it appears on screen it's the best evidence available; his spoken recaps round generously by comparison.
