## The afternoon crash and the jodi pivot
Around midday the market turned into what he called a "ब्लड बाथ" (bloodbath) — Sensex gave up the morning's recovery and kept falling even against good macro prints (8% industrial growth, a 7-month manufacturing high, record GST collections). [▶ 13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFZHFiPxAG4&t=15480s) His line for the disconnect: "लास्ट बुल भी मार्केट से आउट करेगा" (even the last bull gets forced out) — the same capitulation idea from 21-Sep. [▶ 13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFZHFiPxAG4&t=15480s)

As single-side stops ballooned (he explicitly accepted a 50–60 point stop on the 71500 CE, see trade 4, well past the playbook's usual 25–30 point Sensex ceiling), he stopped guessing direction and built two straddle pairs instead, each around ₹120+₹120 combined ≈ ₹240, with a combined SL/target framed as point deltas (SL ~100 points, target ~150 points) rather than per-leg levels. Both closed in profit, the first for ~35 and the second for ~20 points of combined premium. [▶ 12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFZHFiPxAG4&t=11880s) · [▶ 13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFZHFiPxAG4&t=16440s) · frames `031900.jpg`, `043300.jpg`

## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- IV skew as a fear gauge, reinforced: call-side IV climbing while put-side IV dropped early meant "fear is on the call-seller side" that morning. [▶ 10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFZHFiPxAG4&t=5280s)
- "The last bull becomes the bear," reinforced on a fresh crash (first logged 21-Sep). [▶ 13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFZHFiPxAG4&t=15480s)
- News lands on demand/supply zones, reinforced the other way: strong macro prints (GST, industrial growth, manufacturing) landed the same day the market panicked — good news didn't stop the fall. [▶ 13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFZHFiPxAG4&t=15480s)
