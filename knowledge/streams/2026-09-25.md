## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Confluence, defined plainly:** "confluence means getting a few more confirmations to your trade, and the one that supports it is your analysis." [▶ 10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=4860s)
- Standard-deviation/Fibonacci projection tool demonstrated live several times: mark it from the lowest point of the last continuous run of same-colour candles to the highest point that run reached, never from an arbitrary swing. [▶ 09:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=1980s) · [▶ 10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=5220s) · [▶ 12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=14580s)
- "Jab tak close nahi karta kisi ka sagaa nahi hai" (until it closes above/below a level, it belongs to nobody) — repeated as the core discipline for every entry today, on both the CE and PE side. [▶ 10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=6360s)
- Structure shift explained step by step on the 1-minute chart: mark the low-to-high of the last continuous leg that created the reversal, that becomes your projection tool anchor. [▶ 10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=6600s)
- Why he avoided a same-spot re-entry after trade 4's stop: "ek stop loss ke baad ek step peeche aake dekhna hota hai market ko" (after a stop-loss you need to step back and re-read the market before firing again), not simply try the identical trade again. [▶ 15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=21480s)

## Mindset quotes worth keeping
- "Aapka process aapke process ke upar bharosa rakh ke pehla karna hai... strategy strategy kuch nahi hota, sirf observation aapko profitable banata hai" (trust your process; there's no secret strategy, only observation makes you profitable). [▶ 10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=6420s)
- "Ek din mein profit karne se koi profitable nahi banta, ek din mein loss karne se koi loss-making nahi hota" (one profitable day doesn't make you profitable, one losing day doesn't make you a loser). [▶ 10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=6420s)
- On overtrading: "chahe market raat bhar khula rahe, 100 trades bhi de de din ke to bhi kam hi rahenge... market mein ek cheez dekh ke trade karna hota hai" (even if the market gave 100 trades a day it still wouldn't be enough for some people — you trade one clear thing, not everything). [▶ 14:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=20520s)
- "Move miss hua, loss to nahi hua" (a missed move isn't a loss) — his answer to viewers frustrated about not catching every leg on a choppy day. [▶ 15:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yD4AJB3j0e4&t=21240s)
