## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Waiting for a follow-up candle before adding to a running move (core setup A discipline) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlcPnRhXJDs&t=3720s)
- टप्पा (tappa / first bounce): his term for the first bounce off a level, with the second tappa giving a weaker bounce — same idea as "bounce strength" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlcPnRhXJDs&t=10380s) · [▶ second tappa weaker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlcPnRhXJDs&t=10560s)
- Gamma/wall spike selling into a pinned strike: sellers write a combined premium (e.g. ₹30) and sell more into any spike (₹35), explaining why premium kept getting capped near 24,000 · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlcPnRhXJDs&t=19920s)
- Short strangle with a hedge trigger (next month's ATM CE+PE, hedge whichever side eroded first, or hedge once 20% gains show) — a lower-effort seller idea, distinct from the calendar spread he later taught explicitly on 6-Aug · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlcPnRhXJDs&t=9840s)
- Why he avoids Bank Nifty on a day like this: with Nifty and Sensex both moving and Bank Nifty flat, he told viewers to drop the Bank Nifty chart entirely rather than get confused by an unrelated index · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlcPnRhXJDs&t=8100s)
