## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Why breakout candles are not entries; the follow-up candle's high is. [▶ 09:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bp-8w-Hx9w&t=1680s) · [▶ 10:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bp-8w-Hx9w&t=4800s)
- A stop must be defined by the green candle before the entry; a "buy zone" without a candle has no SL. [▶ 11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bp-8w-Hx9w&t=7380s)
- First bounce versus repeated visits (strength decays). [▶ 12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bp-8w-Hx9w&t=13260s)
- Jodi (triangle buying) with equal premiums and a defined loss; exit at break-even when no momentum follows. [▶ 12:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bp-8w-Hx9w&t=13440s) · [▶ 13:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bp-8w-Hx9w&t=16200s)

- Position management for two-lot traders: book one lot at 1:1, trail the second. [▶ 14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bp-8w-Hx9w&t=19140s)
