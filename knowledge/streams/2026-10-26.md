## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Breakout needs a closing candle plus a follow-up; ordinary breakouts are crowded and fail ("two out of five work"). [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=5820s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=14760s)
- Trapping then reversing: break outside a zone, stops get taken, first pin bar. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=15240s)
- Bar-code candle pattern before a strong breakout. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=20940s)
- Mid-market (after ~10:30) is slow; fresh triggers come near the European open. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=7860s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=13200s)
- Equal quantity and risk per trade; small stops, winners 1:3 to 1:5 cover them. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=20160s)
- PCR is a slow indicator; he does not use it. Order flow/delta is used for aggression. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=8160s)
- Overnight: size so a gap against you is survivable, or use a credit spread. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9o0oTA8q8Eg&t=20640s)
