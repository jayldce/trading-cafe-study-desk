## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Stop size as a filter: refuse an entry when the stop is 60–80 pts. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSIAdI3_Gu8&t=1740s)
- Follow-up needed after every breakout candle; large candles without follow-up trap. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSIAdI3_Gu8&t=4980s)
- Pullback zone = base of the strong rally. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSIAdI3_Gu8&t=3120s)
- Ball-bounce rule for the first bounce. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSIAdI3_Gu8&t=2940s)
- Liquidity sweep with pin bar or engulfing. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSIAdI3_Gu8&t=6240s)
- Range day behaviour: act only at range edges. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSIAdI3_Gu8&t=9780s)
- Options must lead spot. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSIAdI3_Gu8&t=8280s)
