### Trade 2 walkthrough (the textbook one)

4. On spot, Nifty was breaking its 23,269–23,276 base toward 23,312, a prior support that had turned into resistance. 23,300 call writers "gave up". [▶ 10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=4200s) · [▶ 10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=6060s)

"If you want to avoid a false breakout, watch the follow-up zone with patience." [▶ 10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=4860s)

## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Breakout candle vs follow-up candle (false-breakout avoidance) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=1320s)
- "Small candles at one level → blast is coming" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=1200s)
- Scalper vs traditional entry: enter directly above the level, or wait for a strong candle and then a pullback · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=1380s)
- Pullbacks need a prior aggressive move (~100 pts on spot wasn't enough) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=2280s)
- Liquidity sweep: price takes the previous zone's lows (stop losses), then buying appears, which gives a bottom-buy with a tight SL · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=6240s)
- Fair-value gaps on the *option* chart trap late buyers or sellers; an "active" FVG makes the move dull · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=6780s)
- Bounce strength: after a level breaks, a weak bounce means a breakdown from the same zone · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=7380s)
- ITM/near-ATM strikes track spot, and high IV helps them · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=5700s)
- Order flow, candle strength and imbalances are read on the option chart; SMC levels on the index · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=7140s)
- Roll strikes when premium runs away or the SL gets too large · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=7980s)
- Ignore the post-3:30 cash candles after a big gap (no serious buyers or sellers present) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTDwaNEZ2RY&t=12780s)

### Method used: his own spot-line method, not Chinmay's option-chart setups
Unlike Chinmay, he draws a single key level (a "black line") **on the Nifty spot chart**, tags it with structure/FVG logic, and waits for a full candle **close** beyond it before entering — never on a wick, over and over: "जब तक क्लोज नहीं करता यह किसी का सगा नहीं है" (until it closes, it belongs to nobody). [▶ 12:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEYKvXFibAU&t=960s) · [▶ 13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEYKvXFibAU&t=2220s). This matches the guest method already logged for 10-Sep (`knowledge/playbook.md` §6): entry/SL/target plotted on spot, the option just prices whatever premium prevails, and profit zones are **standard-deviation projections** (SD1, SD2, SD2.5, SD4) of the latest swing.
