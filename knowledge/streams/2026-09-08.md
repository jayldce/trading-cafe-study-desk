### The "jodi" (long straddle) — new setup, taught live
1. **When:** on an expiry day with a clear trending/volatile tape but no fresh conviction on direction — buy an ATM/near-ATM CE and PE together so a big move on *either* side pays. [▶ 13:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=18420s)
2. **Construction:** 23700 CE (~22) + 23650 PE (~29) = combined premium ~51. SL = a defined ₹ erosion of the combined premium (here ₹25, ≈ ₹1,600/lot); target = combined premium roughly doubling (~₹85–90, ≈ ₹3,000+/lot), a ~1:2 R:R on the pair. [▶ 14:15–14:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=19020s) · frame `053400.jpg`
3. He held through a long, frustrating flat patch (theta bleeding both legs) until the last hour, when the market finally broke — CE 23700 collapsed to ~₹9.60 while PE 23650 spiked; net combined premium reached the 85–90 zone and was booked. [▶ 15:10–15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=22260s) · frame `061100.jpg`, `062000.jpg`

## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Jodi / long straddle** — buying CE+PE together, sized and exited on combined-premium erosion/doubling rather than either leg's own SL. New concept. [▶ 14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=19080s)
- **Volatility skew** — when puts carry visibly richer IV than calls at the same distance, it signals a theoretical directional edge to the downside (put sellers want more premium to compensate for expected downside). [▶ 12:56–12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=13620s)
- **OI/call-writer squeeze, with numbers** — adds a concrete example to the existing "Call writers at round strikes" concept: 23,700 strike OI fell from ~3.79 Cr to ~3.20 Cr as spot pushed through, i.e., trapped writers covering fueled the move. [▶ 12:55–12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=13500s) · frame `045200.jpg` (live option chain)
- **Third test of a level rarely holds** — after two rejections/bounces off the same zone, don't buy the third dip; wait for the level to actually break. Adds to the existing "Bounce strength" concept. [▶ 10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=6660s)
- **"Barcode pattern" / squeeze = no-trade zone** — a run of small, similar-sized candles at one level means buyers and sellers are deadlocked; wait for the range to break rather than trading inside it. [▶ 10:44, 12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=6240s)
- **Confirmation vs. missed trade** — reinforces "breakout vs follow-up candle": he'd rather miss the move entirely than enter before a pin-bar/engulfing confirmation prints, even under visible chat pressure to "just take it." [▶ 10:36, 11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=5760s)
- **Roll to a nearer, similarly-priced strike** when the current one's price action feels too slow to trade cleanly — not just when premium runs away (adds to existing "₹150 near-ATM strikes" / roll rule). Evidence: CE 23600 → 23550 mid-morning. [▶ 10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3r5EJy4cw8&t=4140s)
