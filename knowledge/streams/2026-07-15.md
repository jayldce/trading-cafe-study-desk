### Trade 2 walkthrough (the day's monster move)
1. From the open he tracked both a 77100 CE and PE, then narrowed to a 77500 PE as the put side kept "gearing up" while the call side gave up premium at resistance. [▶ 09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=1620s) · [▶ 12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=12300s)
2. Roughly three early attempts on the same PE around the 250–295 zone got stopped or scratched through the morning and into noon — he says twice he was "frankly trapped" before the real move came. [▶ 12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=12240s) · frame `data/frames/DmPpZPOBuNA/033900.jpg`
3. A clean range breakout finally held. Rather than chase the breakout candle, he waited through several more small consolidation candles for a genuine pullback, explicitly rejecting more than one candle along the way as "not a pullback candle." [▶ 12:38–12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=13140s)
4. Entry came on a small green candle just above the 268 base / ~286 breakout zone he had pre-marked — "यह पुलबैक एंट्री मैं सुबह से ढूंढने की कोशिश कर रहा था" (I've been trying to find this pullback entry since morning). [▶ 12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=13800s) · frame `035000.jpg`
5. Frames confirm the scale of the move: PE premium at ~294 right after entry (12:49), ~300–318 two minutes later (12:51), 600+ by 12:57, and a high above 720 shortly after (12:58) before giving it back to ~540–550 by 13:04. [▶ frames `035200.jpg`, `035700.jpg`, `035800.jpg`, `040500.jpg`]

His own recap right after: three small losses (10-10, 25-30 pts) against two winners, one worth "37 points" and this one worth up to "386 points" at the theoretical peak — he was explicit that nobody actually sits through the full 386. ## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`

- Picking Sensex over Nifty by whichever has the nearer weekly expiry (reinforces the existing "pick the index whose expiry is next" concept) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=3240s)
- Liquidity gabbing vs sweeping — he flags them as meaningfully different reads without fully spelling out the distinction live · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=18240s)
- "Aftershock trades" — small follow-on scalps right after a monster move, worked with reduced size and quick exits · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=15780s)
- One-directional trading vs both sides: he argues being able to trade either call or put (not marrying one direction) is what captures days like this, but only if your mind can handle it without FOMO. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmPpZPOBuNA&t=18660s)
