## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Zone → liquidity take → pin bar/green candle → entry above its high, stop under the sweep (his main method).
- Consolidation break: false break, pullback, continuation (and why a long sideways block traps). [▶ 00:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=1680s)
- Why PCR and OI stopped working for him; expiry-day OI falling means writers covering. [▶ 02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=7320s)
- Trendline break only counts after a liquidity touch. [▶ 02:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=7500s)
- Jodi: equal premium legs, risk = half the combined premium, exit when one leg doubles. [▶ 02:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=9060s)
- Contraction then expansion (inside candles); jodi catches the expansion without direction. [▶ 03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=10800s)
- Two-lot management: book one lot at the small zone, leave the other at entry. [▶ 04:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=16140s)
- Why 5-minute on index, 1-minute on options. [▶ 04:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=17820s)
- News is created to trap retail; he tracks price, not headlines. [▶ 01:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=4260s)
- Scale up slowly; one oversized lot day erases a month. [▶ 05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=19080s)

## Mindset quotes worth keeping
- "Where should I put the stop?": enter only where the stop is small, 15-pt risk against 50–60 pts. [▶ 03:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dDZg2d5JxLk&t=11940s)
