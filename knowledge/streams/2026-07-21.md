## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Pin bar reversal entries: never activate before the pin bar's high breaks · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6kA975_D3I&t=2220s)
- Liquidity/stop-hunt zones taught with a generic 120/110 example, immediately followed by a real aside about a 6–7 pt stop he'd just taken · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6kA975_D3I&t=9600s)
- Theta/time-value explained via a chips-packet analogy: only a few strikes carry real value near expiry, the rest is "hawa" (air) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6kA975_D3I&t=12420s)
- Rolling strikes after a stop, to a cheaper/nearer strike on the same side, to keep risk small and R:R workable · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6kA975_D3I&t=7860s)
- Reading OI stops being useful after 12 PM on expiry day, since falling OI just reflects sellers exiting into decaying premium · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6kA975_D3I&t=13020s)
- Part-booking + trailing as a mandatory discipline once ~10 pts are in favour, illustrated candle-by-candle on the first trade · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6kA975_D3I&t=4680s)
