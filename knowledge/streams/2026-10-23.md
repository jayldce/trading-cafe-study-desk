## Decision moments and rejected setups
- **09:19–09:21, no entry in the first 4-5 minutes.** "Let the market stabilise; the confirmation is an added advantage." Never average an adverse position; if a trade goes against you, do not add. [▶ 09:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=540s)
- **09:37–09:41, why a put today, not a call.** Calls were at the 135 base with an unfavourable ratio; he would look at calls above the day's high (and >146 later). "I take risk if the reward is good, not because it has been beaten so it must turn." [▶ 09:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=1620s) · [▶ 09:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=1860s)
- **09:39 no pullback entry** on the put after the first target; only fresh strength above 227. [▶ 09:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=1740s)
- **10:04 do not bottom-hunt the put** after a one-way panic: wait for a W base; a "dead cat bounce" is a technical bounce, not strong bulls. [▶ 10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=3240s)
- **10:21–10:31 calls on hold:** after the put ran 150+ pts, "it has been beaten so it must turn" is hope trading. Wait for consolidation to be validated by a fresh high; a bottom entry in the call would have been caught in a false reversal. [▶ 10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=4860s)
- **10:51 skipped a continuation** with a 25-pt stop for a 38-pt move; he preferred to wait until consolidation shrank the stop to ~15. [▶ 10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=6060s)
- **11:13–11:22, FVG validity.** A fair value gap exists only after a strong move, judged by the move's strength; and it is valid on the **first** tick back into it, not afterwards. A one-candle V recovery is not a reversal. [▶ 11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=7560s) · [▶ 11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=8040s)

## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Part-book plus trail as a pair; one-lot ladder: target 1 then stop to entry, target 2, etc. [▶ 10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=4380s)
- Do not scale quantity after a winning streak; stick to your lot size. [▶ 12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=10680s)
- Draw a box (position tool) for every entry so the stop drags with you; changing strike every time confuses entry and exit. [▶ 12:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=10500s)
- Why breakouts fail: the crowd sees them and the operator takes liquidity. [▶ 12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=12360s)
- Trend lines are not traded; trend-line breaks without a horizontal level are the most trapping. [▶ 13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=14580s)
- Put sellers at 22,500 panic when the support breaks; reversal is taken from where the first move started. [▶ 11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLVGnIzQs6U&t=8580s)
