- **Liquidity defined:** zones where retail stops sit; big players buy or sell into them. [▶ 10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_veaoignXMQ&t=3600s)
- **Targets:** (1) previous supply/demand zone, (2) Fibonacci (class promised this week). [▶ 10:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_veaoignXMQ&t=4860s)
