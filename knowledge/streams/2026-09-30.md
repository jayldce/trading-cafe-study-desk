## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Fresh distribution point / previous distribution zone as a target: mapping where a prior sharp fall started, then targeting that same zone on the way back up. [▶ 11:34–11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGUAnwhNZkc&t=9840s)
- Order blocks and order flow discussed again as his personal edge on top of plain price action, alongside a live "confluence" explainer (price-action pattern + indicator + a second confirming signal = an A+ setup). [▶ 11:46–11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGUAnwhNZkc&t=10620s)
- A live options-pricing explainer: premium rises from (a) panic/fear (elevated IV) or (b) raw delta from a strong absolute move — and when neither is present, premium "crawls" even while the index barely moves. [▶ 10:13–10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGUAnwhNZkc&t=4080s)
- Risk sizing isn't a fixed percentage of premium ("if the strike is ₹300, my risk isn't automatically ₹30"); he sizes risk from the price-action structure itself, not a ratio of premium. [▶ 12:09–12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGUAnwhNZkc&t=11040s)
- Short covering vs fresh buying: after several failed attempts to sell into a level, the next push through it is partly short covering, which he reads off repeated back-to-back rejection candles. [▶ 12:34–12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGUAnwhNZkc&t=14640s)
- "Market जाता है ऊपर लिफ्ट से, नीचे आता है एलिवेटर से" — his restated version of "stairs up, elevator down": calls tend to grind higher, puts tend to move fast once they get going. [▶ 14:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGUAnwhNZkc&t=20520s)
- BTST caution repeated for expiry eve specifically: only consider carrying a put overnight after 3:15 PM, and only if Sensex is trading below a stated level (70,300 in his words, though this looks like a mis-transcribed 72,300-ish zone given the day's price action) into the close; expiry-day gap risk otherwise wipes the position. [▶ 14:50–14:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGUAnwhNZkc&t=21000s)
