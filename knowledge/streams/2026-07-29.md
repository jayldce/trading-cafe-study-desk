## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Gap-up mornings put IV in a "relaxed", cooler mode — panic-free, but underlying (especially deep-ITM) premiums lag the index move until a real push arrives · [▶ 10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWD9ZCXlChk&t=3660s)
- Gamma blast: near-expiry OTM options with near-zero Greeks can spike violently on a small move because gamma (rate of change of delta) is highest there; sellers do "wall-shot" trading into that spike, and retail should trade the setup they already had, not chase the spike itself · [▶ 13:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWD9ZCXlChk&t=16560s)
- A "reality check" on PCR/OI: pre-2022 selling was simple and OI clearly showed who was doing what; today, expensive margins pushed many traders into **hedged** structures (calendar spreads, one option hedged by another), so the option chain can no longer tell a seller from a hedger. He said PCR is "getting outdated" for scalping and steered viewers away from using it for entries · [▶ 11:04–11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWD9ZCXlChk&t=15840s)
- Multi-strike position sizing: don't scale lots by the raw premium ratio (e.g. "Sensex premium is 2x Nifty's so take half the lots") — the index-times-lot-size weighted average P&L ends up close either way, so 1–2 lots on Sensex behaves like 1 lot on Nifty · [▶ 11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWD9ZCXlChk&t=13140s)
