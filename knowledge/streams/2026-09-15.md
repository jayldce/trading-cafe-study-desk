## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Wait out the first minute of a gap-up before acting — let the opening premium decay first · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=960s)
- "Gap-up ≠ trap": a gap-up only becomes a trap if you force bullish activity into it without confirmation; he traded the put side from the open precisely because the call never showed bullish intent · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=8640s)
- Trading-floor story: two traders with opposite setups can both be profitable at month end — trade *your* setup, not your neighbour's, and don't let a nearby trader's conviction shake yours · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=12120s)
- Short covering, two kinds: stop-loss-driven covering and profit-booking-driven covering — both create a small bounce after a big move, which is why a "dead cat bounce" isn't automatically a reversal · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=15540s)
- Options-for-hedging vs. options-for-trading: he frames hedging as only relevant once a portfolio is large (his example: ₹5 Cr+); everyone else is just trading, not hedging · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=14160s)
- Cash-session timing for jodi entries: premiums stay flat/"stuck" until the 3:15–3:30 PM closing-auction session, so he times the last jodi of the day to catch that late move rather than sit in an expensive jodi all afternoon · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=21900s)
- OTM lottery-ticket math, live example: a ₹10 option needs Nifty to move ~200 points just to stay near zero, and needs the day's close above a specific level to pay off at all — a direct illustration of "near expiry, most premium is air" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JBPc4_cTou4&t=10140s)
