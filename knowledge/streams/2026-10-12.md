## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Spot is analysed with SMC, options are only the vehicle: he does not use FVGs on option charts because options derive from spot. [▶ 10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv82vH6iBzM&t=5460s)
- Timeframe roles: he analyses on 15-minute, Chinmay on 5, and 1-minute is for execution only. [▶ 10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv82vH6iBzM&t=4020s)
- In-the-money strikes on the day before expiry; OTM suffers heavy theta that day. [▶ 10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv82vH6iBzM&t=3600s)
- "Liquidity taken" means the stop losses of sellers and buyers resting at a swing. [▶ 12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv82vH6iBzM&t=11520s)
- Accumulation, manipulation, distribution (AMD): a break out of a consolidation that returns inside is the manipulation leg. [▶ 12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv82vH6iBzM&t=11160s)
- The OI/data shift follows the market and does not lead it. [▶ 11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv82vH6iBzM&t=9180s)
- Low-resistance liquidity gives a V: the market leaves a swept low at the speed it came in. [▶ 10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yv82vH6iBzM&t=6060s)
