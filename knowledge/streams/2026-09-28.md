## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- "आज पूरे नल खुलेंगे" — with no support below, every higher-high entry stays valid; nothing is "missed" on a trend day · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJuzypq8gHA&t=1260s)
- Dead-cat / technical bounce vs a real reversal: a bounce with no fresh trapping below it is a small, capped-target trade, not a trend change · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJuzypq8gHA&t=1920s)
- Live position-sizing walkthrough: ₹20K capital → 2 lots at a ~₹156 entry with a ~6.6-point stop → ₹900 total risk; "if ₹900 scares you, your fear is genuine — size down or go further OTM" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJuzypq8gHA&t=10980s)
- Shakeouts precede real moves and are why he trails by structure (higher-high/lower-low), not a fixed point distance: "जो बड़ी मूव आनी है उससे सबसे पहले जो पुलबैक आता है उसको शेक आउट बोलते हैं... प्राइस एक्शन बेस अगर आप ट्रेलिंग करोगे हायर हाई लोअर लो उस तरीके से तो बहुत बढ़िया काम बनेगा" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJuzypq8gHA&t=12300s)
- Short-covering mechanics, explained live: fresh shorts built on a pullback must be trapped (squeezed) above a level before a real reversal follows — "जब तक ये सेलर्स मार्केट से नहीं निकलते... तगड़ी बाइंग मत सोचो" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cJuzypq8gHA&t=13080s)
