## Jodi (long strangle-style trade) explained
He teaches it as "nothing but a buying triangle": buy a call and a put at about the same price, take the one that doubles, and keep what is left of the other as profit. [▶ 13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az8Ezg6JWks&t=14520s) · [▶ 13:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az8Ezg6JWks&t=14580s)
- **Conditions he names:** premiums cheap (30–35 each at 12:45, 34 each at 13:08), a squeeze/inside range before the move, usually expiry day, and an exit rule on the combined premium. [▶ 12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az8Ezg6JWks&t=11340s)
- **Numbers:** combined 68 (SL 34, "which is high", ₹2,200 at his size; he normally keeps ₹1,200–1,400), target 80–85. Booked at ~85.
