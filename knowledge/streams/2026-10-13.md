## Teaching moments
- **Analysis on 5-minute, execution on 1-minute:** the 143 → 224 move would have had a ~15-point stop from a 15-minute base and about 10–12 on a 1-minute entry; he said he backtested intraday on 12 years of data and that risk-reward on Sensex with a 20-point stop and 70–80 reward is typical. [▶ 11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=9360s) · [▶ 11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=9480s)
- **Targets:** first use previous support/resistance and prior distribution/accumulation zones; in fresh territory (no zone nearby) use Fibonacci extensions. [▶ 09:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=2400s) · [▶ 11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=6960s)
- **Strike choice:** he prefers slightly in-the-money so the risk stays small; ₹60–70 options are out of the money on a slow day and decay as well as lose. [▶ 11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=8640s)
- **Psychology:** he distinguishes a loser who cuts winners early from one who holds for the reward; "be B, not A". Also: on a loss, do not trade to recover it; the market does not know your loss. [▶ 10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=6480s) · [▶ 11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=10200s)
- **Order flow, in one line:** he reads net delta, the changes in it, and where entries are "stuck", as a hint of aggression, not as confirmation. [▶ 13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=15060s)
- **RSI divergence on a 1-minute chart:** useful only if backtested and quantified; RSI is an average of the last 14 or 21 candles, so it never confirms a sharp reversal. [▶ 10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=3780s) · [▶ 11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PoHdfFTi3y0&t=9780s)
