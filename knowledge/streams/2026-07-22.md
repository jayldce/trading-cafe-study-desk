## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Index selection by nearest weekly expiry, not a fixed favourite · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCedaeF9RWg&t=3840s)
- Sensex option liquidity/strike selection is trickier than Nifty's · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCedaeF9RWg&t=18420s)
- Trapping via a double-top: the second, lower high before a break traps more buyers and fuels a bigger counter-move · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCedaeF9RWg&t=4380s)
- A big gap-up candle removes your stop-loss reference for a while — wait for it to settle before planning risk · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCedaeF9RWg&t=10680s)

## Mindset quotes worth keeping
