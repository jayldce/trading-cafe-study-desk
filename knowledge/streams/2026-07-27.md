## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- "Box day" / Test-match market: when price coils in a tight range for hours, sit out rather than force trades — the small losses that day are the cost of capital preservation, not a sign to try harder. [▶ 10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYoKV_bXMD8&t=4320s)
- FOMO after idle screen time: sitting still for 30–60 minutes builds an urge to force a trade the moment ANY candle moves — if not deliberately resisted, it becomes a habit of impulsive entries. [▶ 10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYoKV_bXMD8&t=4680s)
- Aggressive continuation entry (second live example of playbook setup F): when the pullback he's explicitly waiting for never arrives, he'll buy the breakout candle itself with a bigger stop and calls it "aggressive" out loud, rather than pretending it's the patient follow-up setup. [▶ 14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYoKV_bXMD8&t=19560s)
- Dr Lal PathLabs / "ek branch profitable ho tabhi doosri branch" analogy: expand size or strategy only after the current approach is proven and low-risk, echoing a business-scaling story he was reading. [▶ 11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYoKV_bXMD8&t=7680s)
