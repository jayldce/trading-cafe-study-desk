## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Spot-based SL/target, execute on premium**: this host never marks his entry/SL/target on the option chart — he marks them on the underlying (Sensex spot) and buys/sells the option at whatever price is showing the instant spot triggers. [▶ 10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=4260s)
- **Close-confirmation entries only**: never enter on a wick or an early poke through a level — wait for the candle to actually close beyond the marked line, even if it means a worse fill. [▶ 10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=4560s)
- **"Standard Deviation" zones as profit-booking confluence**: plotted from the start of a fresh structural swing to its extreme; used only as a reference for where a move is statistically stretched, not a mechanical system — "प्राइस ही होली ग्रेल है". [▶ 14:43–14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=19660s)
- **Options are derivatives — read the underlying, not the premium, for direction**: premium chasing without reference to spot structure leads to false signals, though he does chart premium for execution/aggression cues. [▶ 11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=6720s)
- **Jodi / combined straddle**: buy a CE and a PE simultaneously (both slightly OTM), sum their premiums, and manage the pair with one combined SL and target rather than two separate legs — e.g. 74900 CE (57) + 74400 PE (48) = ~105 combined, SL 50 combined points, target 100 combined points. [▶ 14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=19260s)
- **When lower-lot capital, use 1:2 risk\:reward strictly — no partial booking**: with only one lot, there's no "half quantity" to book, so it's target-or-stop only, nothing in between. [▶ 14:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=19680s)
- **Don't obsess over a single indicator (like VIX)**: a big fall without a VIX spike doesn't invalidate the move — expectations not being met can suppress VIX; price action is the primary read. [▶ 14:57–14:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v3ieKuYGRc&t=19860s)
