## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- First-time-traded strike on expiry day means thin liquidity and a wide stop; wait. [▶ 10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=4380s)
- Stop-size filter: a stop of 40+ points on an Rs 200–300 strike kills the R:R, so he skips it. [▶ 10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=3420s)
- Cost-to-cost trail after the trade has moved about its stop size. [▶ 09:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=1320s) · [▶ 11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=6300s)
- Jodi (long pair): equal premiums, loss limit and target as combined premium, why it pays on a one-way expiry. [▶ 12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=12300s)
- Breakout vs pullback entry and the confirmation candle. [▶ 12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=12540s)
- Shakeout before the breakout: a zone is made, previous low is taken, price consolidates in the zone, then the pullback. [▶ 11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=8100s)
- Options lead futures, which lead spot; the "work" is in the options and futures, not the spot. [▶ 11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=6060s)
- Rising VIX on a rally means premiums do not decay; sellers do not profit. [▶ 13:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=16320s)
- Expiry-day theta: a deeper in-the-money strike is the way around fast decay. [▶ 11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=7080s)
- Lot-size discipline: raise size only when risk appetite rises with it. [▶ 14:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QaUJ5ei0OF4&t=19680s)
