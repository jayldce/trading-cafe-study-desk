## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Trade price action, not the news/fundamentals: even with a clear FII-selling narrative in hand, he explicitly set his view from price action rather than the headline. [▶ 09:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyvrNoWrQuA&t=1080s)
- FII vs DII "two hands clap" framing for why Indian markets look more panicky on bad days than markets actually at war. [▶ 09:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyvrNoWrQuA&t=1020s)
- Order blocks and when they invalidate: he named a rule in passing — an order block "breaks" (stops being valid) once the market makes a fresh higher high. [▶ 11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyvrNoWrQuA&t=9420s)
- Don't chase a level that's about to be achieved: warned viewers against a late entry right under an already-extended target zone, because the stop-to-reward at that point isn't worth it. [▶ 09:38–09:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyvrNoWrQuA&t=2280s)
- Inside-candle-inside-candle squeeze ("spring" analogy): the tighter a range coils, the sharper the eventual breakout — same idea as the barcode pattern, in a new inside-bar framing. [▶ 10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyvrNoWrQuA&t=5820s)
- Whole-day risk sizing on a big-move day: with 1-minute-timeframe SLs of ~30 points he judged the risk:reward "doesn't work", and stuck to setups where the SL stayed small even as the underlying was moving hard. [▶ 11:24–11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyvrNoWrQuA&t=8400s)
