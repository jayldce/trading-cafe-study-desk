## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Cluster:** three or four candles of consolidation = do not trade inside it; trade only when the cluster breaks, and prefer a smaller stop after a red candle forms inside. [▶ 11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=7080s)
- **Follow-up rule restated:** "unless the breakout has a follow-up, don't participate, or you become the liquidity for people exiting at the top." [▶ 10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=4020s)
- **Stop size dictates quantity:** a 12-pt stop is ₹240 per lot; know that figure before entering; if you cannot stomach it, skip the trade; with a big stop reduce quantity. [▶ 12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=11400s) · [▶ 11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=8280s)
- **5-minute vs 1-minute:** the same zone gives a bigger stop and a bigger target on 5-min (~1:4); 1-minute needs a small candle at the zone. [▶ 10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=6240s)
- **Structure with stairs and elevator:** a fall in steps then a lift to the same zone is a full U; once it completes the move is fast. [▶ 11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=6960s)
- **Bottom hunting:** pin-bar reversals at a swing low are "stop-hunting candles"; a W or rounding structure lets you enter later with a smaller stop. [▶ 12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=10800s)
- **Double bottom's second leg:** the second dip is where people panic out; enter there on the first green candle with a small stop. [▶ 12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=11280s)
- **Theta in a stalled trade:** the 14:06 put sat 22 minutes without reaching the first target; "theta erosion became too large to reach target one". [▶ 14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=19140s)
- **Part-booking and trailing:** with two lots, book one at the first target and trail the second; one-lot traders trail from the start; bring risk to nil after about 1:1. [▶ 13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=16500s)
- **Trade costs:** ~₹40 for one buy and one sell, plus ~₹10–15 STT and charges, so a ₹100 entry breaks even near ₹101. [▶ 12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=12240s)
- **Expiry eve:** ₹300 premiums are "reasonable"; by contrast hero-zero happens on expiry day. [▶ 14:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjb4_5XDOC8&t=18420s)
