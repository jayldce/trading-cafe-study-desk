## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Repeatedly tested resistance becomes weak; the way to trade it is small candles under it so the stop is small and the expansion strong · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJPTZMQLHD8&t=8160s)
- Trade where breakout traders' stops sit, not at the breakout ("90% of traders lose") · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJPTZMQLHD8&t=15060s)
- VIX pull-off days: option gains lag spot; shorter holds and part-booking · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJPTZMQLHD8&t=2280s)
- Option chart moves first, spot follows; do not decide on spot · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJPTZMQLHD8&t=10320s)
- Part-book at 1:1 with two lots, trail the rest to entry; one-lotters trail at 1:1 · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJPTZMQLHD8&t=5280s)
- BTST: carry only a small part and only the risk you can lose Monday · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJPTZMQLHD8&t=17820s)
