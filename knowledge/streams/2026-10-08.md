## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Jodi (long strangle) mechanics on expiry:** buy OTM call + OTM put, combined cost = capital; SL half the combined premium (applied ~3 PM); exit both when one leg doubles; the other leg keeps ₹30-50 so it is net gain; build earlier (about 1 to 1:30 PM) normally, and the later you build, the bigger the move you need. [▶ 11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=9240s) · [▶ 12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=10920s) · [▶ 12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=12360s) · [▶ 14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=18420s)
- **Premium cost drives jodi risk:** near 2 PM he said the legs should have been 80 + 80, not 200 combined; going one strike away just needs a bigger Sensex move (about 200 pts). [▶ 13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=17460s)
- **5-minute vs 1-minute traders:** same zone, bigger stop and bigger target on 5-min (about 60 pts risk for 100); 1-min wants one-candle stops. [▶ 10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=6180s) · [▶ 10:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=5400s)
- **How he picks targets:** an earlier distribution zone or a Fibonacci extension. [▶ 11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=8220s)
- **Fair value gap:** the space between the low/high of the candles around the first aggressive candle after a fresh high and pullback. [▶ 12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=11940s)
- **Consolidation breakouts are usually false; wait for the follow-up** (a candle closing above the zone, then the next candle taking out its high). He does not trade trendline breakouts, only a good support. [▶ 10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=3720s) · [▶ 10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=3840s)
- **Premium erosion:** a PE that does not make a higher high while spot makes lower lows is losing premium; the CE keeps decaying. [▶ 10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=4740s)
- **Stop hunting at the bottom:** he avoids buying the very bottom; he prefers the zone where earlier sellers/buyers are stuck, and a pullback entry with a small candle after that. [▶ 13:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=17280s)
- **Scalping means risk management from below:** trail and part-book; move the stop to entry when the candle goes your way, leave the target to the market. [▶ 13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=15000s)
- **Accuracy vs consistency:** about 50% accuracy is fine with a large risk-reward; consistency is the goal. [▶ 13:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=14760s)
- **Order block + liquidity hunt + pin bar** at the base of a fall is the 13:39 trade's structure (see that trade's why). [▶ 13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kw5L_dY2HPo&t=16080s)
