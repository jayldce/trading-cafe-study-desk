## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Absorption zone** (new term): a range where price fully absorbs supply/demand before the next leg — "148 तक पूरा अब्सॉर्ब एरिया ही है". Distinct from a barcode squeeze in that it's about *why* he ignores a zone, not the entry trigger. [▶ 12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=7260s)
- **Shakeout on the option chart** (adds an example to "Liquidity sweep"): a wrong-side dip on the *option* premium — not the index — designed to run stops before the real move; he explicitly waits for it before entering. [▶ 11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=5220s)
- **Read OI as net change over the consolidation, not the instantaneous number** (adds nuance to the OI/round-strike concept): look at how OI shifted over the last ~30 min relative to the range, since intraday OI is not a leading indicator. [▶ 11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=6780s)
- **IV skew reinforced** with real numbers: calls ~10 vs puts ~7 today (calls richer). [▶ 13:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=15540s)
- **Capital preservation before profit** (new, for beginners): start with small capital, learn the "rules of the game" first; chasing profit before learning usually costs the lesson anyway. [▶ 14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtqPA0aKkzE&t=18000s)
