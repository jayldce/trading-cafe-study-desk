## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Breakout candle → follow-up candle entry, taught live twice more (77200 PE and 76400 CE) · [▶ 09:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=3300s) · [▶ 12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=13080s)
- **Why he avoids indicators (NEW):** RSI/price-based indicators only confirm strength that has *already* passed; he calls RSI divergence "one of the lowest-probability trades". He splits indicators into price-based (RSI, moving averages, stochastic — noise to him) vs volume-based (ADX, VWAP, raw volume — his preferred confirmation tools if used at all). A tight multi-candle squeeze (NR7/NR4-style) is read as "a blast is coming". [▶ 10:50–11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=6600s)
- **Breathing reset for FOMO/anxiety (NEW):** 10–12 slow deep breaths with eyes closed, then ask yourself "what's my next thought going to be?" — a deliberate pattern-interrupt he says clears ~99% of trading anxiety before re-engaging with the chart. [▶ 11:45–11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=9900s)
- Options chart leads, spot follows — reinforced again, don't watch spot on a 1-min chart or you'll get confused. [▶ 10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vK9x0XEwaAg&t=4260s)
- Jodi (long straddle) construction, with real numbers this time: pick a CE and PE each around ₹70 combined premium ~₹140, SL is half the combined premium (~₹70, ≈₹1400 for a 20-lot), target is roughly double the combined premium.
