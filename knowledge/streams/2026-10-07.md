## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- IV rises into scheduled news and drops after it: avoid straddles/strangles right before the event; do them on expiry-volatility days · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=2580s)
- Event-day behaviour: first move is often a false breakout, then a hard reversal; wait for the event, then read structure · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=3420s)
- No news trading: when commentary is bad look for long setups, when it is good expect profit booking; setups rarely align with sentiment · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=10020s)
- Tight consolidation = false-breakout trap; take breakout candle + follow-up, and never a pullback in a "no-pullback" tape · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=1560s)
- Fair value gaps form at bottoms after a strong move and a retest; they are not drawn on spot at this stage · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=4560s)
- "Tussle-muscle": when CE and PE both fight at key zones, wait, then join the winner · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=9600s)
- Barcode candles (zigzag) mean fast moves, so plan a small-risk entry · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=9420s)
- Pick the strike by premium (~₹300; drop to a cheaper strike if the SL is too wide) and re-set strikes after the move · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=5700s)
- Trailing ladder: when primary target touches, SL to entry (cost-to-cost), then trail candle by candle; with a 15-pt SL "I don't panic" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=8640s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=9060s)
- Max about three trades a day; two big back-to-back losses end the day; volatile days are the exception · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=7140s)
- Buyers trapped in the last leg after a liquidity sweep when the zone does not turn · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F-PdJ_DvsDY&t=11220s)
