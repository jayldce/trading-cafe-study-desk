## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Two entry tiers off the same level: a tighter "scalper" trigger vs. a further, momentum-confirming trigger — reused explicitly today for the CE. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxy5ftj5Z6U&t=1620s)
- "No pullback, wait for a clear higher-high after a breakout attempt" stated as his personal rule for avoiding false breakouts — applied to the Sensex PE (and it still lost). [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxy5ftj5Z6U&t=17280s)
- Volatility skew explained from the option seller's side: sellers demand a richer premium on whichever side they expect the move to be on, which is what tilts IV toward calls or puts. [▶ 13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxy5ftj5Z6U&t=17700s)

## Mindset / discipline notes
- After a stop, "discipline ≠ accuracy" — take the setup, cut the SL if hit, and only re-engage once real momentum returns; don't force a second or third attempt at the same level. [▶ 10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxy5ftj5Z6U&t=6240s)
- On a viewer's ₹2.5 lakh loss: don't trade with a "recovery" mindset — treat each trade as a fresh opportunity, use losing periods as a "pit stop" to fix process, not chase the money back. [▶ 12:22–12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxy5ftj5Z6U&t=11040s)
- Second/third test of the same zone carries less strength than the first ("दूसरे टप्पे में उतना दम नहीं रहता") — explained with a bouncing-ball-losing-energy analogy; don't buy the repeat test, wait for the level to actually break. [▶ 12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gxy5ftj5Z6U&t=11220s)
