## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Gamma spike:** a premium spike that pushes an option's price above what spot's own move justifies; he said "wall traders" use such spikes to *sell into*, not to chase. NEW concept. [▶ 14:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=19080s) · [▶ 14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=19140s)
- **Don't assume a winning streak is skill:** if your first trade wins and you don't know whether it was logic or luck, your second trade is "most probably still random" — resist the urge to size up. [▶ 10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=3600s)
- **Aggressive vs pullback entry, restated:** when the ideal pullback zone doesn't arrive, taking the aggressive alternative means accepting a bigger stop for the same targets — a live example of the nuance already logged for setup B. [▶ 10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=6120s)
- **IV crush is structural on expiry day**, not a signal — trade price action, don't read too much into IV that day. [▶ 10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=5580s)
- **Capital-sizing rule of thumb** for small accounts (₹10–15k): expect ~40–45 trades over a month, size risk so the capital survives even if most of them lose, and let the market decide the upside. [▶ 13:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=16980s) · [▶ 13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=17160s)
- **Why Bank Nifty is skipped most days:** low liquidity and wide ranges outside month-end make his usual scalp risk/reward not work there. [▶ 14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sj_SDOjZFSI&t=17520s) (context remark, not a trade)
