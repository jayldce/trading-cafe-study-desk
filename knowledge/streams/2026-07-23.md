## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- No trigger order on a breakout — wait for the breakout candle to sustain, then the follow-up candle, before entering · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uu-LJo9Li30&t=2760s)
- Confirmed reversal entry: wait for a strong green pin bar to actually print, then enter above it with that candle's low as the SL, rather than buying the first dip · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uu-LJo9Li30&t=8580s)
- Jodi construction on Sensex expiry afternoon: CE + PE roughly equal premium (₹40 each here), combined SL at half the combined premium, combined target at double it · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uu-LJo9Li30&t=17880s)
- "Index doesn't drive options, options drive the index" — his end-of-day mindset one-liner, consistent with his usual "option leads, spot lags" teaching · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uu-LJo9Li30&t=21600s)
- Sizing by rupee risk on Sensex: a ₹300-400 option with a 10-15pt SL on a 20-lot is a small, manageable risk even though the premium looks big · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uu-LJo9Li30&t=22140s)
