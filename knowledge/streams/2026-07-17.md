## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- **Apex** — the zone a breakout launched from, where price often returns for one calm pullback before the follow-up leg · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5wgTKROoOhc&t=9480s)
- Squeeze/barcode → expansion is coming, same idea as later streams' "small candles at a level → blast" · [▶ 09:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5wgTKROoOhc&t=1320s)
- Sustain-above-level entries vs chasing the poke through a level · [▶ 09:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5wgTKROoOhc&t=2400s)
- IV skew example: put IV (14) vs call IV (9) that morning · [▶ 09:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5wgTKROoOhc&t=1680s)
