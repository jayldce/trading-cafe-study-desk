## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Chop/box-day discipline: even trying to control it, a dead-range day pulls him into 25–30 small trades, which he calls a sign something's wrong · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syl2Kx5emtQ&t=19680s)
- Don't force size into a non-moving market — "जब मार्केट नहीं मूव हो रहा है तो हम क्यों ज्यादा अग्रेशन लेके चले" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syl2Kx5emtQ&t=3900s)
- IV skew read live: PUT side IV 17–18 vs CALL side 12–13, pointing at richer downside pricing that morning · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syl2Kx5emtQ&t=12240s)
- Prefer a costlier, more liquid premium over a very cheap one near expiry — the cheap one's small stop looks attractive but it rarely delivers the bigger move · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syl2Kx5emtQ&t=9960s)
- First candle of the session differs by broker/platform because of differing tick-capture timing — don't read anything into day-open wicks across broker charts · [▶ 09:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syl2Kx5emtQ&t=3120s)
