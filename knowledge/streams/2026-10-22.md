### Why he took each trade
1. He wanted a fair-value-gap / double-bottom style zone where "the previous candle's high and the previous candle's low" gap sits and price comes back to it. He had pointed at the 283 zone at 09:54 ("when the market makes a high and then a double-bottom-like zone forms under it"). Price swept the lows ("it took the liquidity, then turned"), he risked a single candle (below ~234, about 45 pts), entry above the follow-up at 279–280, T1 ~350 then 450 / 562. After the trade he said the setup came from "very simple price action": a strong distribution zone above, a fair value gap he had named, liquidity taken, then a one-candle stop.
