## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Zone-to-zone trading: enter where the market first stalls, take risk small, ride the next zone. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=3720s)
- Expansion vs contraction phases of a market. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=1800s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=4980s)
- Distribution/accumulation zones work 1–2 times, then weaken. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=4800s)
- Jodi mechanics: sell the leg that doubles, the other leg's leftover premium is the net gain; stop on combined premium (≈ half). [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=11280s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=16320s)
- Fixed rupee risk, variable lots: the closer the stop, the larger the lot. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=7020s)
- He does not use OI, PCR, moving averages or EMA: "pure naked price action". [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=2940s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=4680s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=15600s)
- Entering where others' stops sit (liquidity), then getting a panic move. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=20700s)
- Part-book with two lots: one lot booked, the other to cost and left open. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=6780s)
- Two to three good trades is a good day; do not re-enter in mid-market consolidation. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=5820s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=11040s)
- BTST on expiry-eve: use slightly in-the-money options or spreads. [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnCas9JOTJI&t=8220s)
