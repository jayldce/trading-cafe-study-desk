## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Breakouts are crowded trades: everyone sees the same level, so buy the first pullback or the follow-up, not the breakout candle ("we become the liquidity that buys the top") · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=4020s)
- Strength comes with a trap: a stop-hunt at one end, then the far side gets trapped; once traders see the trap the move doesn't stop easily · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=4200s)
- "Fresh resistance": after a fresh low forms from a consolidation, the upper level of that consolidation is fresh resistance, and it must be cleared before puts can show strength · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=4680s)
- Risk-reward decides reversals: with a 13–14-pt stop you need ≥30 pts; a bottom-buy with 6 risk vs ~14 reward (about 1:2) is barely acceptable · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=5160s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=7260s)
- Strike choice: ~₹150 gives the usual 5–8 pt stop with real targets; a ₹70–80 strike has the same stop but the reward shrinks to the same 6–8 pts · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=3300s)
- Position sizing: 1 lakh means 2 lots while learning, at most 5 lots even when experienced · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=720s)
- How he uses OI: not during trends. Only in a 30–45 min sideways window, checking whether put-writers are adding and call-writers removing at 23300/23350; overall PCR (0.91 → 0.93) is polluted by OTM hedges · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=5460s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=13740s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=13860s)
- Market phases: dead markets favoured sellers (2017–18), momentum and high VIX favoured buyers and jodis (2020), and the last 1–1.5 months favoured expiry-day jodis until Thursday's premium melt-down · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=6660s)
- Spot vs option: Nifty moved a lot more than the options did; options hadn't even reached the earlier distribution zone, so the move wasn't reflected in premium · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=12900s)
- Futures aren't for him: quit after 2022–23 because breakeven takes ~16 pts after STT · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=9420s)
- Credit spreads: he prefers them above ~10 lakh of capital; between 1 and 10 lakh you can go either way; below that, small-lot option buying · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=8100s)
- Morning tight range often means momentum in the second half (or vice versa), but on this day the "delta" was the lowest he'd seen in a month · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPh5qxWEUhU&t=15480s)
