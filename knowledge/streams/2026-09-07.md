## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Roll to a cheaper/fresh strike right after a stop-loss instead of re-entering the same one — keeps risk small while staying with a working read · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=2340s)
- Reading IV skew across CE vs PE to see which side option sellers fear more that day · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=2400s) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=12120s)
- Synthetic future / put-call parity: a deep-ITM put and a deep-OTM call on the same expiry can trade at oddly close premiums because both price off the same synthetic future · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=18960s)
- Multi-lot scaling ladder: with 2+ lots, book ~1 lot around 1–1.5x initial risk, trail the rest to breakeven, then trail under each fresh higher-high · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=8340s)
- Dead cat bounce: a small reversal candle inside an oversold/overbought move that fails to hold — needs a strength check, not just "first green candle" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=18720s)
- Breakout trigger levels: for a trap-the-sellers entry, set a hard trigger price and don't act until price actually crosses it, rather than anticipating the move · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=16560s)
- Minimum 1:2 risk-reward before sizing a trade; he explicitly reasons in rupee-risk-per-lot terms before entering · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=14820s)
- Never pyramid (add quantity) into a working option-buying scalp — pyramiding belongs to swing trades, where a position can turn into a longer investment · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=10860s)
- Bond-yield read: Japanese bond yields matter as much as US yields for Indian flows, since Japan is a major funding source for FII money into India · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=480s)
- Sensex vs Nifty option behaviour: with only 30 base stocks vs Nifty's 50, Sensex option premiums can move faster for a similar index-point move · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=19080s)

## Mindset quotes worth keeping
- "एक बार पार्ट बुकिंग और ट्रेलिंग कर ली, कैपिटल रिस्क पे है क्या?" — once a trade is trailed to breakeven, stop worrying about exactly where it ends. [▶ 09:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=3300s)
- On being told the market is at his mercy: "व्हाई इट विल प्ले विद यू? ओनली इफ यू विल एंगेज विद इट" — the market only "punishes" you if you force entries where there's no setup. [▶ 11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=11520s)
- Don't chase FOMO re-entries; the same lesson from 11-Sep repeats here almost word for word, and the one late re-entry he did allow (trade 6) gave back a small loss. [▶ 14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=16620s)
- On accuracy: he keeps a personal trading journal and openly reports his own losing trades (11-point loss to start the day) alongside the wins — used to model journaling discipline for viewers. [▶ 13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=14400s)
- A first-time viewer's first-ever trading profit prompted an explicit warning: the *next* trade after a first win is the dangerous one, because new traders often oversize risk right after a win. [▶ 14:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhSkNorpW6w&t=18240s)
