## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Reading the wick: when a 15-minute candle has already printed a wick, enter the 1-minute candle that catches it; "a big move has small wicks and a big body" · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=15360s)
- Double-side liquidity (both highs and lows of the last 15-minute candle taken) makes the market choppy; trap both sides · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=12120s)
- Standard deviation tool = Fibonacci retracement settings changed to expansion levels; plot it from the low to the high of the reversal (three candles if the low was built by three) · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=6420s)
- Risk-reward discipline: losses of 2-7 points, wins of 15-27; one trade should cover several stops · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=7620s)
- After profits, risk only the profit: split the day's gain across 2-3 trades · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=900s)
- Part profit booking: single-lot traders book at 1:2; multi-lot traders take 50-60% at target and trail the rest · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=3720s)
- Premium decay hedge in sideways markets is a spread; selling needs capital · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=6420s)
- Jodi only on expiry day with ~25-30 rupee options; not on a morning like this · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=5880s)
- Fair value gaps are not all filled; the "every gap fills" idea is a myth · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=16080s)
- Back-testing: he says dynamics shift every ~6 months so a three-year back-test does not carry over · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61JX6X_6c-g&t=15180s)
