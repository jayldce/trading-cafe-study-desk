## Concepts taught today → see `knowledge/concepts.md`
- Order blocks: the last opposite-colour candle before a fresh move is where aggressive orders sit; price returning there often bounces. [▶ 10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=7140s)
- Liquidity vs. plain resistance: liquidity specifically means "where stop losses sit"; sweeping it is different from just re-testing a supply/demand zone. He drew a live example distinguishing the two. [▶ 12:29–12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=12527s)
- Order flow / limit orders: big traders build positions quietly with limit orders at levels where retail stop losses cluster; retail tends to chase with market orders. [▶ 11:28–11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=8867s)
- Jodi (straddle) construction formula, explicit this time: combined premium ≈ ₹25, SL = half (₹13), target ≈ double; manage each leg's exit independently, no trailing stop on a jodi. [▶ 13:59–14:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=17927s)
- Daily trade quota: 2–3 trades a day; if it's back-to-back stop losses, close the terminal for the day rather than force a 4th. [▶ 13:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=17327s)
- Realistic expectations / risk-reward math: fixed monthly % targets (e.g. 10%) imply near-impossible accuracy at 1:2 RR; think in quarters/years, not daily P&L. [▶ 11:16–11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hkQBf4EvCuo&t=8147s)
