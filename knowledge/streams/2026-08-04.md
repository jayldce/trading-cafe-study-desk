### Jodi (long straddle) — the day's main teaching block
He spent nearly 40 minutes (12:29-13:05ish) teaching the jodi mechanically, live, with real numbers:
- **Construction:** buy a near-ATM CE and PE together. First pair: 24550 CE (~₹29 falling to ~₹9-25 through the day) + 24450 PE (~₹17 rising to ~₹30-62). Combined entry ≈ ₹45-46. [▶ 12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmnPW3HauhM&t=11340s) · [▶ 12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmnPW3HauhM&t=11460s)
- **Risk:** stop-loss = half the combined premium (~₹20, ≈ ₹1600-1700 for one lot). **Target:** roughly double the entry (₹70-75 combined, ≈ ₹2500-3000 net). [▶ 12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmnPW3HauhM&t=11520s)
- **Management:** exit both legs together, whichever side "blasts." He explicitly said don't manage the legs separately — "we're not betting on call or put, we're betting on profit." [▶ 14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmnPW3HauhM&t=18000s)
- **Outcome:** combined premium climbed through the afternoon — ~46 → ~51-52 (13:57) → ~58 (14:57) → ~60 (14:58, where he told viewers to book). His own closing recap: "यह जोड़ी सुकून वाला था ... 3000 का जोड़ी था, ₹1000 का यह प्रॉफिट दे रहा था" (a ₹3000-capital jodi that gave ₹1000 profit, > 30%). [▶ 14:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmnPW3HauhM&t=17520s) · [▶ 15:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zmnPW3HauhM&t=21720s) · frame `055300.jpg` (24450 PE leg alone shows LTP 62.34 around this time)
