## Lesson of the day
A gap down onto the prior day's low does not reverse at that low — it reverses at the next pool beyond it. Wednesday's low broke by 85 in the first minute; the turn came at the 5-session low (a 4.1-point sweep at 09:35), and the tradeable signal was the reclaim of Wednesday's low 37 minutes later. The rally then stopped at the first tick of the day's own opening gap. On a range day both kinds of gap fill: the rules' sweep reversals paid, and four of five gap pullbacks did not.
