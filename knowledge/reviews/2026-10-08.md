## Lesson of the day
When the opening print is the high and the first level breaks in the first minute, the day has told you its side — and on a trend day the useful levels are the ones *above* price, where the bounces stall. Each pullback on Thursday turned inside the gap the last impulse had left (22,372–22,396, 22,303–22,322, 22,268–22,279); the one long the rules found, a sweep of a round number against eight down impulses, lost.
