## Lesson of the day
A gap day that holds its gap does not have to trend. Monday's +169 stayed over 23,200 and still gave a 153-point round trip inside the bars, and the side that paid best was the put, on a day whose gap said calls. What separated his one loss from his biggest win was 23 minutes and one candle: at 12:35 he bought a zone under a fresh down gap, and at 12:58 he waited for the pullback into a down gap and bought the put, the same structure read from the other side.
