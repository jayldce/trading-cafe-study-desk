## Lesson of the day
A gap down does not reverse at the first pool it takes — it reverses at the pool beyond the ones it takes in its first candle. Tuesday's low, 22,600 and Monday's equal lows all went at 09:15 without a turn; the low came an hour later at 22,500, inside Monday's open launch gap. The recovery then stopped exactly where gap-fill logic says it should — Tuesday's close — and the one candle that rejected it was the day's second-best rule trade.
