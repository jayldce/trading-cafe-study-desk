## Lesson of the day
A gap down that sweeps the nearest pool and closes back above yesterday's close in the same candle has told you its side in the first minute. From there each level it broke — Wednesday's high, 22,800, the equal highs — held on the first pullback, and the only rule trade that paid was the first short of the one dip, because a trend day's pullbacks fill their own counter-gaps.
