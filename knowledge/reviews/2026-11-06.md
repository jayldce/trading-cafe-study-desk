## Lesson of the day
**The first candle is not the trend.** Friday's opening minute fell 130 points, which on its own reads like a gap-down day; it was a sweep of 24,400 into Thursday's open gaps, reclaimed within 18 minutes, and the low of a 562-point rally. The level it reached was on the prep. The second lesson is the box: **three hours under 24,700 with equal highs building at the top** is the market telling you where the next leg starts, and the rules' four losing signals were all taken *inside* it or against the break out of it. On a trend day, the trade is the pullback into the break's gap (G4), not the gaps inside the range.
