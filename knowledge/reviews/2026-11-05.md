## Lesson of the day
**A gap that opens above your whole map leaves you with no map.** Every level in Thursday's prep — the 24,000 round number, the 23,945.70 high, the four open gaps, the 23,700 floor — was below the first tick and stayed irrelevant for 375 minutes. The levels that mattered were the ones the session made itself: the opening range, broken at 09:45, and the high it built at 13:48. The second lesson is the shape of the move. 283.6 points, one direction, and the rules found exactly one trade — in the last hour, after the trend had already stopped. A grind without pullbacks is not a bad day for the method; it is a day the method has nothing to say about, and the discipline is to notice which one you are in before you force an entry into it.
