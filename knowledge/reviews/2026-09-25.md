## Lesson of the day
Equal highs stacked above a range are not resistance; they are a queue of stop orders. Three sets built up over four hours (10:12/11:21, 11:51/12:30, 13:00/13:12/13:45), and once the 12:57 break of the lows failed and was reclaimed, all three were taken in four minutes. The map to carry from a quiet box is *where the stops are*, and which side just failed.
