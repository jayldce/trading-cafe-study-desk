## Liquidity sweeps and pools
- **The first-minute sweep of 23,100 had displacement the wrong way.** It closed back above 23,100, but four minutes later the index fell 205 points through it. That sweep was a pause in a fall that had not started yet. It was not a floor.
- **The pool that mattered was the day high's twin.** At 09:37 the rebound reached 23,168.45 and could not take 23,175.25. The buy stops above the high were left untaken, and every later level broke downward.
- **Friday's low 22,913.80** was poked by 7.8 and reversed with displacement at 13:03 (the S2 shape). That reversal still failed 14 minutes later, so on a trend day a sweep plus a reversal candle is not enough.
- **Untaken pools left for Wednesday:** the equal highs 22,957.15–22,961.25 and 22,899.3–22,903.7, and the equal lows 22,845.00–22,845.25 (the day low).

## Fair value gaps that mattered

## Lesson of the day
On a trend day the open bear gaps are the map: every bounce after 10:30 stopped inside one. The two exceptions were the first 15 minutes, when an expiry open's gaps filled both ways, and the 13:03 sweep reversal, which failed in 14 minutes. The highest-quality short of the day needed no rule at all. It was the 09:37 rebound that ran out of buyers 6.8 points under the day high, leaving that high's stops untaken.
