**In one line:** the morning gap filled all the way to yesterday's low, which was swept twice and held; the afternoon high swept the opening-range high and held too. Everything in between was range.

## The map at 9:15
- Yesterday (15 Sep) was a 436-point trend day down that closed at its low: high 23,588.30, low and close **23,118.60**.
- The open (23,201.05) was an 82-point gap up, inside yesterday's huge range. The first minutes spiked to 23,281 and dropped straight back.
- Opening range: **23,188.30–23,281.05**.
- Bias (lesson 5.2): inside yesterday's range after a trend day — a range day. *Between 23,118 (yesterday's low, the gap fill) and 23,281 (the opening-range high) it's a range; act at the edges.*

## Liquidity sweeps and pools

- **Opening-range high 23,281.05, 13:09:** the mirror image at the top — poked by 3.7 points and rejected.
- **Equal lows 23,206–23,210** (12:03, 13:42): the afternoon floor, still untaken at the close.

## Lesson of the day
After a big trend day, the next session often ranges between the previous day's extreme and the new opening range. Both edges were swept and both held. The money was at the edges; the middle was noise.
