## Lesson of the day
Monday and Tuesday both opened on the wrong side of the previous day's low and never reclaimed it — but Monday trended away from it and Tuesday ranged under it. The level told you where the ceiling was on both days; it did not tell you how far price would travel. On Tuesday the trade came from the box's structure instead: equal highs broken on a big candle, and the gap it left used as the floor.
