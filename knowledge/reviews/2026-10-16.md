## Lesson of the day
A gap above the prior high that loses that high in two minutes is not a long open, whatever the bias score says — it is a gap fill until something is swept. Today the something was the opening-range low, and the reversal candle had to be 2.2× normal before the long was a trade. After the day's two displacement moves (09:40 up, 10:38 down) the box held for four hours; on a Friday before an expiry Monday, that is what a range looks like from the inside.
