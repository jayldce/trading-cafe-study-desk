## Lesson of the day
A gap up into the range of a trend day down is a test of the prior high, not a new trend. When it stops short — 54 points under Monday's high here, 6.8 under it on Monday's own rebound — the open gap becomes supply: the opening range broke, the failed break of its low gave one clean long (G1), and after that every bear gap held and both sweep longs failed. On days like this the bull gaps fill and the bear gaps stay open; trade the side the open gaps are on.
