## Lesson of the day
**A range day fills its own gaps — trade the one decisive candle and leave the rest.** Nine of ten gaps filled; the one rule trade that worked (G2) came straight after the single candle that changed the morning, the failed opening-range break at 10:10. The other side of it: **a gap up off the open is not the same as a fade continuing.** Wednesday's five open bear gaps looked like a ladder down; Thursday's first minute took the low and the first hour filled three of them.
