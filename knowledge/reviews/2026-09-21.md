## The 13:03 signal: why a "sweep" wasn't one

This is the most instructive trade of the day, because the rule was wrong and the reason is learnable.

```
12:50   23,410 – 23,414
12:54   23,414 – 23,418        price living ABOVE 23,410 for 30+ minutes
13:02   23,412 – 23,415
13:03   23,415 → 23,406        one down candle → the rule called it a sweep + reversal
```

The scanner shorted 23,406.75 with a stop at 23,418.25 and was stopped 13 minutes later. Price then rallied 60 points to the day high.

Two things the rule failed to account for:

1. **The level had already flipped.** Those equal highs were broken at 11:54 and price was *accepted* above them for over an hour. A level price has conquered and is resting on is support, not overhead liquidity. A sweep rejects within a candle or two; half an hour of acceptance is the opposite of a sweep.
2. **It was counter-trend.** By 13:03 Nifty had put in a higher low at 23,397 and was making higher highs off the 09:15 low. The 13:03 dip was a retest of the flip, and it held.

The lesson generalises: **once price closes beyond a level and holds it for 20+ minutes, retire that level as a pool.** The pullback into it is a continuation entry, not a reversal.
