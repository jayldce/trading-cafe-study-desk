## Lesson of the day
A new high that fails without a reversal candle tells you only in hindsight. The morning's top had no rule signal, and the level everyone could see (Tuesday's close 22,721.95) was crossed back and forth for three hours. The day's decisive information came from **displacement**: the 12:51 and 12:58 impulses left bear gaps that never filled, and from that moment the map was down to 22,600 — the same "gaps with the move stay open" rule as the trend days, arriving at 1 p.m. instead of 9:15.
