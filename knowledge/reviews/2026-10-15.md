## Lesson of the day
**The first minute set the day.** Nifty's low and Sensex's low both printed at 09:15, and six of the bull gaps the trend left by 11:20 were still open in the last bar. On a day like that, the rules' insistence on a deep pullback buys nothing in the morning. His own entries show the alternative: he took small pullbacks on the *option* chart, each sized off one candle. The afternoon taught the reverse. Once the 13:40 impulse down printed, a long was the trade against the latest displacement, and the rules (G3, G2 on Sensex) and his 14:04 put both went with it.
