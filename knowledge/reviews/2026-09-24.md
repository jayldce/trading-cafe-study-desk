## Lesson of the day
A big gap down that prints its high in the first minute is not a reason to buy the dip. The morning box looked like support at 23,200, but it had no sweep with displacement and a lower high already locked in by 09:38; the real signal was the 11:56 break of the week's low, and after it every pullback into a bear gap was a place to sell, not a turn.
