## Lesson of the day
The prep's map was right and the scanner was wrong, on the same chart. The gaps listed the night before were the day's path (22,648 → 22,703), and the turn came at the far edge of the last one. Once price had arrived, the move was over: the next three hours were a box around a round number, and a rule that buys gap fills sells you both sides of a box. When a session reaches its mapped target before noon, the afternoon setups need a reason beyond the rule.
