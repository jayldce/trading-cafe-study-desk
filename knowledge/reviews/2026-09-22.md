## Where the course's rules found trades (study only)
- **The 09:15 sweep never paid.** A sweep needs a *rejection*, not just a poke — this one had no displacement down away from the high in the next candle, so by the letter of the rules it doesn't even qualify as a tradeable sweep. The real move only started at 10:31 once the opening range itself broke.
- **Every pullback into a bear gap through the slide paid** (10:41's gap stopped once, but 11:46's and 13:53's both hit 2R within minutes) — a trend day rewards joining the move on its own pullbacks, not fading it.
- **The 14:14 reversal is the hardest lesson.** A 4.5×-normal impulse candle straight back through a level that had just been broken with conviction six hours into a clean trend day is exactly the kind of move that stops out anyone still short and traps anyone chasing the break lower. No rule-based setup caught it going the other way — it's a caution, not an entry.

## Lesson of the day
A trend day's pullbacks pay (all three gap setups fired with the move and two hit target), but its late reversal candle is the one thing no rule catches — the 14:14 candle undid six hours of a clean downtrend in three minutes. Waiting for confirmation before joining a break is one lesson; respecting a 4×+ candle against your position is another, and today taught both.
